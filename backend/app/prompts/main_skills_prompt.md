# Chapter Method Synthesis Task

You are a methodological analysis specialist.

Your task is to synthesize a **chapter-level method summary** and **signal summary** based on all chunks within a chapter, using the provided `raw_pattern_chain` and `method_signal_chain`.

---

## Requirement

- The generated **main_skill** must be directly usable to guide downstream writing.
- Therefore, the `method_program` must NOT be a descriptive summary of the chapter’s method, but a **procedural, executable description of how to carry out the reasoning process**.

---

## Input

You will be given:

- `raw_pattern_chain`: an ordered list of raw_pattern entries
- `method_signal_chain`: an ordered list of methodSignals

---

## Tasks

### 1. pattern_summary

Based on `raw_pattern_chain`:

- Extract the **core methodological essence** of the chapter (`description`)
- Specify **what types of problems this method is suitable for**, and MUST describe **how to use the method to answer such problems** (`applicability`)
- Derive **3–6 core steps** (`core_steps`)
- Construct a **method flow structure** that reflects sequential progression (`pattern_flow`)
- Generate a **coherent paragraph summarizing the full reasoning procedure** (`chapter_method_summary`)
- MUST use a **procedure-style expression** (instructional), not a descriptive summary of the author or chapter

Requirements:
- Do NOT concatenate or restate raw_pattern entries
- You MUST perform abstraction and compression
- Prioritize recurring patterns; ignore one-off details
- Emphasize **how the method progresses**, not what the content is

---

### 2. signal_summary

Based on `method_signal_chain`, summarize each field globally:

Each field must include:

- `value`: the dominant value for the chapter (must be selected from predefined options)
- `notes`: a one-sentence description of how this field behaves across the chapter  
  (e.g., consistently stable / shifts over time / progressively intensifies / locally varies)

Fields to summarize:

- perspective
- nature
- time_orientation
- system_scope
- equilibrium_view
- logic (value must include 1–3 dominant reasoning types)

Requirements:
- Do NOT list chunk-level values
- You MUST perform global aggregation
- `notes` must describe **patterns of variation or stability**, not restate the value

---

### 3. confidence

Provide a float score between 0.0 and 1.0 indicating the reliability of the synthesized chapter method.

Consider:
- consistency of patterns
- stability of signals
- clarity of the method procedure

Guidelines:
- 0.8–1.0: highly consistent and clear
- 0.5–0.8: moderately consistent
- 0.0–0.5: fragmented or weak

Output only the number. No explanation.

---

## Expression Examples

- ❌ Incorrect (descriptive style):
  - The analysis begins by establishing...
  - The author first traces...

- ✅ Correct (procedural / instructional style):
  - First establish the original principles or evaluative baseline,  
  - then trace the historical development path,  
  - next identify internal contradictions generated by prior success,  
  - finally connect these contradictions to observed outcomes to explain how systemic crises emerge endogenously.
